The Founding Vision

"I took a blanket from my car and wrapped him in it, and the toothless smile he gave me filled my eyes with tears. I had to pull over... he told me I was the first person he had interacted with in six months."

"Driving home felt so empty thinking of how many stories and life experiences he might have that no one will ever get to hear or read or even know about."

That’s why I created this platform: to give a voice to those whose voices have been silenced by age or circumstance.
