The Encounter That Changed Everything

"In the heart of winter on a snowy and freezing day, I drove past a homeless man totally covered sitting outside. Though he was trembling, his eyes flickered with a warmth that touched me. I had to pull over."

"I took a blanket from my car and wrapped him in it, and the toothless smile he gave me filled my eyes with tears. I went back to my car, however, I could not shake his face or his gratitude in telling me I was the first person he had interacted with in six months."

"Driving home felt so empty thinking of how many stories and life experiences he might have that no one will ever get to hear or read or even know about. That incident was three years ago, but it’s not left my mind since."

That’s why I created this platform: to give a voice to those whose voices have been silenced by age or circumstance.

Do you know a voice that needs a home?

Whether it is your own story or the story of an elder in your community, we are here to listen.
